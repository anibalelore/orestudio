## How It Works
1. **Kick‑off** – The **Director** defines the brief.
2. **Strategy** – The **Strategist** designs a data‑backed plan.
3. **Research** – The **AudienceResearcher** uncovers insights.
4. **Create** – The **Writer** drafts copy, the **VisualProducer** builds assets, while **ImageReferenceAgent** and **VideoMemeAgent** supply visual inspiration.
5. **Polish** – The **QualityEditor** refines everything.
6. **Publish & Engage** – The **SocialManager**, **TikTokAgent**, **InstagramAgent**, and **CommunityManager** launch content, track performance, and interact with fans.
7. **Optimize** – The **GrowthAnalyst** evaluates results and suggests the next iteration.
